# 1. Vision

StudyHub is an AI-powered web platform designed to help students prepare for international studies.

It centralizes within a single environment:

- Scholarship search
- Application tracking
- Administrative document organization
- AI-assisted English learning
- Technical skill development
- Interview and language exam preparation

The goal is to replace dozens of scattered applications with a single coherent and intelligent platform.

---

# 2. Context

Each year, thousands of African students abandon their dream of studying abroad due to difficulties such as:

- Lack of reliable information
- Poor organization of application files
- Insufficient English level
- Missed deadlines
- Lack of personalized mentoring

Today, these tasks require multiple different tools:

- Google Drive
- Notion
- Duolingo
- ChatGPT
- Google Calendar
- Excel
- University websites
- LinkedIn
- GitHub

StudyHub aims to group all these functionalities into a single platform.

# 10. Long-Term Vision

StudyHub is more than a study management application.

It is envisioned as a complete educational ecosystem that helps students prepare academically, linguistically and professionally for international opportunities through the power of Artificial Intelligence.

Ultimately, the platform could integrate:

- A scholarship recommendation engine based on academic profile
- An AI capable of automatically reviewing application files
- Mock admission and scholarship interviews
- Personalized preparation paths for English exams (IELTS, TOEFL, Duolingo English Test)
- A space for connecting students, alumni, universities, and mentors
